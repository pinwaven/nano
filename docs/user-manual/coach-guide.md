# Coach Guide

This guide covers the **Coach Panel**, available inside the WeChat Mini Program to any user with the `coach` role. Open it from the menu (tap the header/logo) → **Coach Panel**.

The coach role can be held alongside other roles (e.g. a user account can also be a coach), and is tied to whichever channel your assigned clients belong to.

## Overview

The Coach Panel has five tabs: **My Clients**, **Invites**, **My Earnings**, **Questionnaires**, and **CRM**. A menu in the header also lets you jump to your own personal Health view, the Kino Simulator (if you also hold an admin role), theme/language toggles, and logout.

## Your Client List

The **My Clients** tab lists everyone assigned to you, showing their BioAge vs. chronological age (with an up/down indicator) and the date of their last Kino test. Use the search box to find a client by name, or the filter chips to narrow by CRM stage (see [CRM](#crm-pipeline-appointments--activity)). Tap a client to open their detail view.

## Client Detail: Health, Plans, Chat, Messages

Opening a client shows several sub-tabs:

- **Health** — the client's biomarkers, BioAge, and sub-age breakdown (same data shown to the client themselves on their Health tab).
- **Plans** — the client's active health plans, with adherence/check-in stats. From here you can recommend a plan or create a custom one (see below).
- **Chat** — a read-only view of the client's conversation history with their AI companion.
- **Messages** — send the client a direct message from you (labeled as coming from their coach, distinct from the AI).

## Notes, Goals & Personal Facts

Additional sub-tabs let you track a client over time:

- **Notes** — free-text notes about the client; pin important ones to the top, or delete ones no longer relevant.
- **Goals** — set a target (BioAge, a specific sub-age, weight, steps, sleep score, HRV, or a custom goal) with a target value and date. Goals show as active, achieved, or missed.
- **Facts** — a record of durable personal facts (dietary restrictions, allergies, preferences, or other goals) the client has mentioned, either recorded automatically from chat or added by you manually.

## Recommending or Creating a Health Plan

From a client's **Plans** sub-tab:

- **Recommend Plan** — pick from the existing plan library to suggest to the client.
- **Create Custom Plan** — build a plan tailored to the client, specifying a name, goal, and duration in weeks.

## Setting Reminders

From a client's detail view, tap **+ Set Reminder** to compose a reminder: enter the content, choose a date and time, and optionally set it to repeat daily or weekly.

## Using the Chat Toolbox on a Client's Behalf

The same tools available to end users in chat — **Upload Image**, **Formulate Dots**, and **Health Advice** — are available when messaging a client, letting you trigger a formulation or a health analysis on their behalf. (Note: unlike the end-user app, this view has no background notification polling, so longer AI analyses are run with an extended timeout and deliver their reply directly in the conversation rather than via a separate notification.)

## Invite Codes

The **Invites** tab lets you generate your own invite codes to share with prospective clients. Each invite can carry an optional note (e.g. "WeChat group A" or "Spring campaign") to help you remember its purpose. You can copy an invite's link, edit its note, or deactivate it (which invalidates any copies already shared).

## Questionnaires

The **Questionnaires** tab lists questionnaires available to assign. Tap **Assign** to select one or more clients to send it to. Once a client responds, their answers appear under that questionnaire's response list.

## CRM: Pipeline, Appointments & Activity

The **CRM** tab gives you a pipeline view of your clients across stages — **Lead**, **Onboarding**, **Active**, **At Risk**, **Churned**, and **Graduated**. Drag or select a client to move them between stages.

From here you can also:
- **Schedule an appointment** — give it a title, date, time, and format (video call, phone, in-person, or WeChat).
- **View recent activity** — a feed of everything logged against your clients (messages sent, reminders set, plans assigned, Kino scans, stage changes, notes added, appointments, goals set/achieved, and bulk messages).

## Your Earnings

The **My Earnings** tab shows your pending commission for the current month, your available withdrawal balance, and a history of past payouts with their status (pending approval, approved, or transferred).

## Your KPIs

A metrics summary shows your total and active client counts, clients needing attention, this month's commission, your NPS score, how many Kino tests you've helped facilitate, and which clients have improved the fastest.
